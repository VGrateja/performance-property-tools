    📖 How to play

    Tiles outlined in green are the ones you're placing this turn; a violet glow marks the last word played.

    1 Your first word covers the centre

    The opening play must place at least 2 tiles and cross the centre ★ square.

    C3 A1 T1

    2 One straight line, no gaps

    Every tile you place in a turn must sit in a single row or a single column, with no empty squares between them.

    Allowed
    W4O1R1D2
    Not allowed
    W4O1D2
    gap in the line

    3 Connect to what's on the board

    After the first move, your new tiles must touch at least one tile already on the board.

    Allowed
    C3A1T1S1
    adds to CAT
    Not allowed
    C3A1T1 D2O1G2
    floating, not touching

    4 Real words only

    Every word your move creates — the main word and any words formed alongside it — must be in the dictionary.

    Valid
    C3A1T1
    Invalid
    X8Q10J8

    If any word isn't valid you forfeit the turn — your tiles come back and you score 0.

    5 Premium squares boost your score

    2LDouble letter
    3LTriple letter
    2WDouble word
    3WTriple word
    ★Centre = 2W

    A bonus square only counts on the turn you place a NEW tile on it. Letter bonuses apply to that tile; word bonuses multiply the whole word.

    6 Blanks & the 7-tile bonus

    E a blank plays as any letter — but scores 0
    S1T1A1I1N1E1D2 all 7 tiles in one turn = +50

    7 Swapping tiles (standard)

    Trade any 1–7 tiles for fresh ones from the bag. Needs 7+ tiles left in the bag, and it uses your turn.

    8 Free swap — keep your turn

    Stuck with duplicates? If you hold 3 or more of a letter you can swap them and keep your turn. You may mix any letters you hold 3+ of — e.g. two I's and one O when you have 3+ of each.

    I1I1I1 O1O1O1 swap 2× I + 1× O, free

    Blanks can't be free-swapped; a free swap just needs enough tiles in the bag to redraw.

    9 Pass & resign

    Pass skips your turn. Resign takes you out: with 3–4 players the game continues without you (you're greyed out and skipped); in a 2-player game it ends and your opponent wins.

    10 How the game ends

    The game ends when a player uses their last tile and the bag is empty, after six zero-score turns in a row, by draw agreement, or when only one player remains.

    At the end each player loses the value of the tiles left on their rack; whoever emptied their rack gains everyone else's leftovers.
