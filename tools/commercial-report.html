Medical

Medical and allied health tenants demonstrate a high resilience to economic downturns when compared to tenants in discretionary spending sectors. This resilience stems from the necessity of healthcare, and government support. Health practices often require specialised infrastructure and equipment, resulting in costly and complex fit-outs. This creates a strong incentive for tenants to remain in their existing locations, reducing tenant turnover even during economic challenges.
