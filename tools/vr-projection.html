Vacancy Rate Projection

Where each market's vacancy rate is heading over the next 1–2 years — incoming supply (approvals & commencements) weighed against incoming demand (population growth → new households). Lower VR = tighter rental market. Directional, not precise.

Method: VR = surplus properties ÷ (occupied households + surplus). The forecast adds the region's expected new households over the year (population growth: natural increase + net interstate + net overseas migration ÷ household size) and incoming stock (approvals + dwelling commencements), then recomputes the vacancy rate. The 2-year forecast repeats this with a further year of supply/demand. Read it for the direction of supply vs demand rather than exact levels. Rent forecast = current rent + rent-slope × forecast VR change. Data from the VR Projections workbook → rdp_vr_forecast. Use the VR source toggle to anchor the projection on the SQM vacancy (default) or the CoreLogic/Cotality house vacancy — the demand and supply drivers are identical, so only the starting VR and everything downstream (forecast VR, surplus, rents) change.
