How it works

  • One rule. Every row, column and 3×3 box holds 1–9 exactly once. Boss stages (every 25th) are X-Sudoku: both long diagonals must hold 1–9 too.
  • One ladder. Stage 0 is the tutorial. After that each stage opens when you clear the one before it, and everyone plays exactly the same grid.
  • The clock is the server's. It starts when you press Play and stops only while the board is hidden. Pause (or P) covers the board and stops the clock; leaving pauses it for you — closing the tab, switching away, going back to the map, or losing the connection. Resume shows the board and the clock runs on. Pausing is free and your time still ranks; rows with a pause carry a small pause mark. Restart deals a shuffled grid with a fresh clock.
  • Penalties. With auto-check on, each wrong digit adds 0:30. A hint reveals a cell for 1:00, three per attempt. Penalties count in your time and are served before the next stage opens.
  • Per stage. Every stage keeps a top 5 by time: gold, silver and bronze for the three fastest. The first player to clear a stage wears its crown for good. Beat the stage's par for a star.
  • Reading the map. Teal tiles are the stages you've cleared, with your best time and medal. A gold crown marks a stage someone has cleared (on your own tile, only if the crown is yours), and a short gold rule sits after the highest stage anyone has cleared. Pink is the stage open for you now.
  • Replays and restarts. Replay a cleared stage to improve your time. A restart or a replay shuffles the grid (rows, columns and digits swapped), so it plays exactly the same but can't be typed in from memory.
  • Overall. The highest stage cleared leads. On the same stage, whoever got there first ranks higher.
  • Daily puzzle. A fresh grid every day at midnight (Melbourne time), the same for everyone and outside the ladder. Play it on the day: one attempt, no restart (Clear wipes your entries, the clock keeps going). Top 10 each day, with gold, silver and bronze for the fastest three.
  • Weekly sprint. Five puzzles each week, Basic to Master, Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (Melbourne). Play them in any order; each puzzle has its own clock, from the moment you open it, and it stops while its board is hidden. Your five times add up to one total, and the fastest total wins the week — the Sprint winner badge is yours for good.
  • Streaks. Clear any puzzle — a stage, a replay, the daily or a sprint puzzle — on consecutive days to build a streak. Badges at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days are kept for good.
  • Hint tokens. Clear a new ladder stage without a hint and you bank a token (up to 5). The next ladder hint then costs nothing; the three-hints limit still applies. Tokens don't work on the daily or the sprint.
  • Colours. Switch the pad to Colours (or press C) to shade cells while you think. Colours are yours alone, saved with your board, and never count as answers.
